Zine Concept

I designed a total of four collages for this zine, one for the artist statement spread, and then three others each representing one of her songs. The songs I chose were: Born to Die, Fine China, and Black Beauty. I decided on using collages as a medium for this zine because I felt that collages are the easiest way to express a vivid and wild imagination.

Born to Die

One of my all-time favorite Lana del Rey songs is Born to Die, a lush, cinematic elegy to a doomed love. In the world this zine explores, Born to Die, is a painting too, not just a song. The fictional myth of this painting is that no one knows who painted it, but it depicts Lana del Rey as a goddess amongst the clouds.

Fine China

The second exploration uses Lana’s tender and heartbreaking song Fine China, and turns it into a Hollywood film. The song is about a bride whose groom leaves her on the day of their wedding. Thus, the movie is the story behind that.

Black Beauty

Black Beauty is a sweeping, grand song about the pain of loving someone who can’t find happiness. This time, instead of Lana herself being the star of the medium, she is the creator. She is the author of a novel about two reunited wartime sweethearts, where the man cannot escape his haunting memories of the war. The woman is left to stand by him and support him, but even she cannot reach where he is.
