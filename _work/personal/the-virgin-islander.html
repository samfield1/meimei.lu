Finding the Photos

One of the fun challenges of this magazine was to find models to represent the characters from the novel. The novel has not been adapted into a movie or a TV series, so it was completely up to me to find models who matched their description. I scoured social media platforms to find models, looked through all of their available photoshoots, and I ended up lucking out with the content I found.

Writing the Content

Since this is a lifestyle magazine, I followed a popular format found in those types of magazines, which is that the feature article is of an interviewer asking questions to a celebrity. In this case, the fictional interviewer is interviewing the main character of Land of Love and Drowning, Anette Bradshaw.

Other Required Sections

The project also required for us to design a full-spread advertisement, write a one-page article, and design a back-cover advertisement.
