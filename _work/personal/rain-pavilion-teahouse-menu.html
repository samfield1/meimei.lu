Adding in My Illustrations

Before I added in my illustrations, the menu looked almost too clean. It didn’t communicate the lush and soothing interior of the actual teahouse. I did a bit of research on what kinds of plants are common on Chinese porcelain, and decided to draw small persimmon branches.
