--- layout: work-main priority: 10 title: Artifice Street subtitle: UI/UX design, video game design year: 2024-2025 type: Senior Thesis Video Game description: > Artifice Street is a video game I created for my graphic design senior thesis project. In this game, users interact with different virtual storefronts and then play activities and mini-games that teach different aspects about graphic design. My senior thesis was about helping the general public more fully appreciate graphic design in the world around them. After interviewing several professional designers about what makes graphic design stand out to the general public, I gathered that it was a matter of teaching people. Thus, Artifice Street aims to teach users, in a fun and engaging way, about just how much the world needs graphic design." ---
