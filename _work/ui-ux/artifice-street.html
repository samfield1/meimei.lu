The Challenge

For this video game, I had to teach principles of graphic design without making it too educational. If the video game was just a matter of reading things about graphic design, that’s no better than just telling someone what they should think and believe. I wanted the video game to help the user arrive at new understandings about graphic design that they can’t get from just reading. They have to interact with the game.
