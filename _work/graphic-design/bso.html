Frozen Concert Print Mailer

One of the most exciting projects I was in charge of designing was the Frozen in Concert print mailer. This project required a focus on the composition and arrangement of the assets and copy I was given to work with. Typography, hierarchy, and spacing all had an impact on the result. The final product was mailed to all subscribers of BSO.

Bethesda Magazine Full Page Ad

This project was a full page ad in the Bethesda Magazine, for the 2024-2025 season of BSO. I was responsible for arranging the photographs that recapped all the categories of concerts BSO had in a season, as well as adhering to branding guidelines and perfecting typography.

Concert Series Key Art

One project where I was really able to utilize my personal style was the key art for one of the BSO’s concert series called Music Box. These concerts are meant to introduce the world of classical music to children. With this being the theme, I combined one of my key skills, which is collaging, with targeting a young audience to design these key art collages. I themed each concert key art to have a central color from the brand guidelines, and used collage elements to reinforce the central color theme.
