About Me

My name is Meimei Lu! I am a recent graduate  with a BFA in graphic design at the George Washington University. I have designed my own video game, branding work for the Baltimore Symphony Orchestra, and had my work exhibited at the Hirshhorn Museum of Modern Art in Washington, DC.

Graphic design began for me when I was 12.  I began designing graphics about fictional characters from my favorite books and movies. From then on, my mission has been to express beloved stories and emotions through graphic design.

Outside of design, I love music. I was born  an illustrator. I’m deeply connected with  my Chinese culture. Ask me about Attack on Titan, Naruto, or the Persona games. I’m currently training Taekwondo, Aikido,  and Kenjutsu.
